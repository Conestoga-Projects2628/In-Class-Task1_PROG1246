Call of Duty(Overview)

Call of Duty (CoD) is a legendary first person shooter video game franchise launched by Activision in 2003. Initially focusing on intense, cinematic battlefields of World War II, the series achieved massive global stardom with the 2007 release of Modern Warfare, shifting the genre into contemporary combat settings.

Today, the franchise continues to dominate the entertainment space by rotating through different historical eras, near-future sci-fi settings, and traditional black-ops operations. Its free-to-play battle royale installment, Warzone, maintains millions of daily players and has secured Call of Duty's footprint in global esports. With annual high budget releases, smooth gunplay, and a highly competitive online community, it stands as one of the best selling video game properties of all time.
